<h1>Internal Quality Assurance Cell</h1>
<h2 id="about-iqac">About IQAC</h2>
<p>Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) of the College is constituted for ensuring quality sustenance and enhancement as a continuous process. The prime task of the IQAC is to develop a system for the conscious, consistent and catalytic improvement in the performance of the institution. IQAC consists of representatives of all the stakeholders – parents, staff, students and management. The IQAC channels the efforts and measures towards the academic excellence of the Institution and assures its stakeholders the accountability of the Institution for its own quality. Besides this, it also disseminates information regarding the quality bench marks and parameters of higher education through organizing seminars and workshops. IQAC undertakes responsibility for the systematic documentation of various activities of the college and preparation of Annual Quality Assurance Report to be submitted to the NAAC.</p>
<p>An effective feedback system envisaged by IQAC facilitates policy making process in accordance with the changing scenario thereby ensuring the quality maintenance of the Institution.</p>
<p>IQAC is constituted under the Chairmanship of the Head of the institution with heads of important academic and administrative units and a few teachers and a few distinguished educationists and representatives of local management and stakeholders</p>
