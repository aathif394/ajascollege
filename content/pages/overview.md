<h2 id="al-jamia-arts-science-college-ajas">Al Jamia Arts &amp;
Science College 
(AJAS)</h2>
<p>Al Jamia Arts and Science College, located in Poopalam, Perinthalmanna, Kerala, is a distinguished institution dedicated to providing quality higher education. Established in 2010, the college is affiliated with the University of Calicut and operates under an unaided status, maintaining its financial independence as a self-financing institution.</p>
<p>The college offers a wide array of academic programs to cater to diverse student interests and career aspirations. It provides ten undergraduate (UG) programs, encompassing various fields of arts and science, ensuring a comprehensive education that equips students with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills. Additionally, the college offers two postgraduate (PG) programs, further allowing students to specialize and deepen their expertise in their chosen disciplines.</p>
<p>With a focus on academic excellence and holistic development, Al Jamia Arts and Science College is committed to fostering a nurturing environment that encourages learning, innovation, and personal growth. The college’s affiliation with the University of Calicut ensures that its curriculum is rigorous and up-to-date, meeting the high standards set by the university.</p>
