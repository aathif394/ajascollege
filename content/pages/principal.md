Al Jamia Arts & Science College is Self-Financed college affiliated to the University of Calicut in the Perinthalmanna constituency in Malappuram district, Kerala. If we work in harmony with insight and determination, the materialization of our goal is not a distant dream. The achievements that we have gained and the infrastructural facilities that we have set up within just few years itself are the evidences of it. I welcome all of you to this wonderful institution with your valuable suggestions and sincere prayers. So, let’s unleash our vigour and enthusiasm and make miracles. Established in 2010, the college is marching ahead with a sense of direction and positive energy gained from its stakeholders and well-wishers to reach to the front line of the best institutions.
