<h2 id="about-placement-cell">About Placement Cell</h2>
<p>The Placement Cell acts as a platform that bridges the industry and the students of the College. It plays a crucial role in locating job and internship opportunities for Under Graduates and Post Graduates during the campus placement by keeping in touch with reputed firms and industrial establishments. It operates round the year to facilitate contacts between companies and graduates. The number of students placed through the campus interviews is continuously rising. On invitation, many reputed industries visit the institute to conduct interviews.</p>
<p>The Placement Cell organizes career guidance programs for all the students starting from the first year. The cell arranges training programs like Mock Interviews, Group Discussions, Communication Skills workshops., etc., and it also organizes Public Sector Exam Training for students who are interested to join Government Sectors.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<ol><li>To organize campus recruitment drives in collaboration with reputed organizations.</li><li>To conduct training programs in soft skills, aptitude, and interview techniques.</li><li>To provide career counseling and guidance for higher studies and entrepreneurship.</li><li>To strengthen linkages between the institution and industry for better placement opportunities.</li><li>To ensure the highest possible placement rate for all eligible students.</li></ol>

<h2 id="vision">Vision</h2>
<p>To facilitate holistic student development by enhancing employability and ensuring successful career opportunities through strong industry–institution collaboration.</p>
<h2 id="mission">Mission</h2>
<p>To equip students with professional competencies, communication skills, and industry-relevant knowledge through continuous training, career guidance, and placement support.</p>
