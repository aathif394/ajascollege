<h2 id="about-nss">About NSS</h2>
<p>The NSS Unit of Al Jamia Arts and Science College functions in alignment with the National Objectives of the National Service Scheme, fostering the spirit of voluntary service and social responsibility among students.</p>
<p>With over a hundred energetic and enthusiastic volunteers, our unit is a vibrant community of young minds dedicated to creating meaningful change in society. Each volunteer brings unique skills and perspectives, united under one vision to serve humanity and build a better tomorrow.</p>
<img src="/assets/uploads/2024/06/nss1.png" alt="" class="img-fluid">
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<ul><li><p>To promote a spirit of selfless service and social responsibility among students.</p>
</li><li><p>To provide opportunities for students to understand community needs and contribute to their upliftment.</p>
</li><li><p>To develop leadership qualities, team spirit, and discipline through active participation in service activities.</p>
</li><li><p>To enhance value-based education and moral awareness among the youth.</p>
</li><li><p>To encourage environmental protection, health awareness, and national integration.</p>
</li><li><p>To shape students into socially committed and empathetic citizens.</p>
</li></ul>
<h2 id="major-programs-activities">Major Programs &amp; Activities
</h2>
<ul><li><p>Rural Development Programs</p>
</li><li><p>Village adoption projects</p>
</li><li><p>Charity and Service Programs</p>
</li><li><p>Health and Care Initiatives</p>
</li><li><p>Skill Development &amp; Leadership</p>
</li><li><p>Environmental &amp; Awareness Campaigns</p>
</li><li><p>National Integration &amp; Cultural Activities</p>
</li></ul>
<img src="/assets/uploads/2024/06/nss-1024x684.jpg" alt="" class="img-fluid">
<h2 id="our-vision">Our Vision</h2>
<p>To inspire youth to serve the community with dedication and compassion, building a generation that values humanity above all.</p>
<h2 id="our-motto">Our Motto</h2>
<p>“Not Me, But You” – Reflecting the essence of selfless service and collective growth.</p>
<p>National Service Scheme popularly known as NSS, the scheme was launched in Gandhiji’s Centenary year in 1969, and the first NSS unit in our college was started in the year 2011 as Unit no. KL 099. As the whole we have 100 volunteers in the unit. As per the guidelines the NSS volunteers should complete 240 hours of regular works and a seven-day special camping which is necessary for the successful completion of volunteer ship. Being an active member these student volunteers would have the exposure and experience to be the following: an accomplished social leader, an efficient administrator, a person who understands human nature.</p>
