<div class="content-blog-single-wrap migrate-content"><h2 id="about-lab">About Lab</h2>
<p>An Artificial Intelligence (AI) lab is a dedicated space where students and faculty work on AI-related projects, research, and experimentation. These labs provide an environment where theoretical knowledge about AI can be applied to real-world problems. They typically focus on fields like machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing (NLP), robotics, and data science.</p>
