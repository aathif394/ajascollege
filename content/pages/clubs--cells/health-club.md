<h2 id="health-club">Health Club</h2>
<p>The primary objectives of Health Club are to promote physical fitness, enhance mental well-being, and encourage a healthy lifestyle among our students. We aim to provide a structured and supportive environment where students can pursue personal health goals through guided exercise, balanced nutrition, and wellness education. The Health Club offers a variety of programs including strength training, yoga, aerobics, and personalized fitness plans.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>The aim of the Health Club is to create awareness among students for maintenance of good physical and mental health. Students are sensitized regarding health, nutrition and maintenance of a healthy lifestyle.</p>
