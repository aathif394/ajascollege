## About

## Health Club

The primary objectives of Health Club are to promote physical fitness, enhance mental well-being, and encourage a healthy lifestyle among our students. We aim to provide a structured and supportive environment where students can pursue personal health goals through guided exercise, balanced nutrition, and wellness education. The Health Club offers a variety of programs including strength training, yoga, aerobics, and personalized fitness plans.

## Objectives

The aim of the Health Club is to create awareness among students for maintenance of good physical and mental health. Students are sensitized regarding health, nutrition and maintenance of a healthy lifestyle.

## Mission

Increase health awareness by providing health screenings, activities, materials, demonstrations, and information. To develop all aspects of oneself physical, social, mental, spiritual. To develop a wellness awareness within our community
