## About

## Ethics Cell

The Ethics Committee of the college is established to uphold and promote the highest standards of integrity, honesty, and ethical conduct among students, faculty, and staff.

It ensures that all academic, administrative, and research activities of the institution are carried out in a fair, transparent, and responsible manner.

## Objectives

1. To uphold ethical standards in teaching, learning, and research.
2. To promote awareness of moral values and professional integrity.
3. To monitor and prevent academic and research malpractices.
4. To implement and review the institutional Code of Conduct.
5. To address ethical issues and recommend corrective actions.
