<h2 id="ethics-cell">Ethics Cell</h2>
<p>The Ethics Committee of the college is established to uphold and promote the highest standards of integrity, honesty, and ethical conduct among students, faculty, and staff.</p>
<p>It ensures that all academic, administrative, and research activities of the institution are carried out in a fair, transparent, and responsible manner.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<ol><li>To uphold ethical standards in teaching, learning, and research.</li><li>To promote awareness of moral values and professional integrity.</li><li>To monitor and prevent academic and research malpractices.</li><li>To implement and review the institutional Code of Conduct.</li><li>To address ethical issues and recommend corrective actions.</li></ol>
