<h2 id="about">About</h2>
<h2 id="anti-narcotics-cell">Anti-Narcotics Cell</h2>
<p>The motto of the Anti-Narcotics Cell is to educate and empower our students to lead a healthy lifestyle and to actively participate in preventing substance abuse. Our aim is to develop awareness, responsibility, and leadership qualities that enable students to make positive life choices and influence their peers. On a campus where academic pursuit is balanced with extracurricular activities, fostering a drug-free environment ensures that the well-being and safety of every student is protected. Having an anti-narcotics cell helps keep students informed about the dangers of substance abuse throughout the academic year. With limited time in the classroom to address these challenges, the Anti-Narcotics Cell acts as a critical supplement—offering workshops, outreach, and support for those seeking guidance. It also creates opportunities for students to champion healthy living and actively participate in building a supportive campus community.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>The main objective of the Anti-Narcotics Cell is to raise awareness among students about the harmful effects of drugs and to encourage them to participate in prevention activities. The cell works to equip students with knowledge and life skills, empowering them to resist peer pressure and lead by example. It seeks to develop leadership abilities in organizing awareness campaigns and outreach programmes. The Cell also aims to promote a healthy, secure, and supportive atmosphere for academic and personal growth. By participating, students enhance their sense of social responsibility, improve their decision-making skills, and boost their mental health and confidence</p>
<img src="/assets/uploads/2024/05/Frame-1.svg" alt="" class="img-fluid">
<h2 id="vision">Vision</h2>
<p>To foster a drug-free campus where students can thrive academically, physically, and emotionally in a safe and supportive environment. The Anti-Narcotics Cell envisions empowering every student to make informed choices, reject substance abuse, and become responsible citizens who contribute positively to society. Through sustained awareness initiatives and collaborative efforts, the cell aspires to build a culture of prevention and self-discipline among the student community</p>
<h2 id="mission">Mission</h2>
<p>The mission of the Anti-Narcotics Cell is to promote a drug-free campus by educating students about the dangers of substance abuse through awareness programs, counseling, and preventive initiatives. By fostering responsibility, supporting those at risk, and encouraging positive peer influence, the cell strives to create a healthy and vigilant college community</p>
