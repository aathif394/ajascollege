<div class="content-blog-single-wrap migrate-content"><h2 id="about">About</h2>
<h2 id="counseling-cell">COUNSELING CELL</h2>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<ul><li>Promote self-awareness, confidence, and healthy coping skills among students.</li><li>Provide guidance for academic, emotional, and interpersonal challenges.</li><li>Prevent and manage issues like stress, anxiety, depression, and addictions.</li><li>Foster positive relationships and interpersonal skills.</li><li>Organize programmes on mental health and life skills.</li><li>Support the Jeevani Project and premarital counselling initiatives for youth well-being.</li></ul>
<img src="/assets/uploads/2024/05/Frame-1.svg" alt="" class="img-fluid">
<h2 id="vision">Vision</h2>
<p>To foster the holistic development and mental well-being of students by providing a supportive environment that enhances their personal growth, emotional stability, and social adjustment.</p>
<h2 id="mission">Mission</h2>
<ul><li>Promote students’ mental health and emotional well-being.</li><li>Provide counselling support for academic, personal, and social issues.</li><li>Conduct awareness programmes on stress, substance abuse, and relationships.</li><li>Offer crisis intervention and support for students in distress.</li><li>Foster a positive and inclusive campus environment.</li></ul>
