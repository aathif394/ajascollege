## About

## COUNSELING CELL

## Objectives

- Promote self-awareness, confidence, and healthy coping skills among students.
- Provide guidance for academic, emotional, and interpersonal challenges.
- Prevent and manage issues like stress, anxiety, depression, and addictions.
- Foster positive relationships and interpersonal skills.
- Organize programmes on mental health and life skills.
- Support the Jeevani Project and premarital counselling initiatives for youth well-being.


## Vision

To foster the holistic development and mental well-being of students by providing a supportive environment that enhances their personal growth, emotional stability, and social adjustment.

## Mission

- Promote students’ mental health and emotional well-being.
- Provide counselling support for academic, personal, and social issues.
- Conduct awareness programmes on stress, substance abuse, and relationships.
- Offer crisis intervention and support for students in distress.
- Foster a positive and inclusive campus environment.
