<h2 id="malayalam-vedhi">MALAYALAM VEDHI</h2>
<p>Aims to promote and preserve the richness of the Malayalam language, literature, and<br></br>
culture among students.<br></br>
– “MASHI “- Conducts activities like Malayalam poetry recitation, story writing,<br></br>
reading sessions, debates, and cultural discussions.<br></br>
-Organizes celebrations for Malayalam Day (Malayalam Bhasha Dinam) and other<br></br>
cultural festivals related to Kerala traditions.<br></br>
– Encourages students to express ideas and creativity in their mother tongue, fostering<br></br>
pride in regional heritage.<br></br>
-Helps enhance language skills</p>
<img src="/assets/uploads/2024/05/Frame-1.svg" alt="" class="img-fluid">
<h2 id="vision">Vision</h2>
<p>To preserve, promote, and celebrate the richness of the Malayalam language, literature, and culture by fostering a community of learners who take pride in their linguistic heritage and creative expression.</p>
<h2 id="mission">Mission</h2>
<p>The mission of Malayalam Vedhi is to inspire a deep appreciation and love for the Malayalam language and its rich literary heritage. The club seeks to create a vibrant platform for students to express their creativity through writing, reading, and cultural activities conducted in Malayalam. It aims to nurture linguistic pride, promote the beauty of native expression, and preserve the cultural essence of Kerala while encouraging innovative engagement with the language in modern contexts.</p>
