<h2 id="debate-club">DEBATE CLUB</h2>
<p>The debate club provides a dynamic platform for students to develop their public speaking, reasoning, and analytical skills.it encourages participants to engage in thought-provoking discussions and structured debates on contemporary issues, fostering confidence and clarity in expression.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>-To enhance communication and persuasive skills.</p>
<p>-To encourage healthy debate and respect for diverse opinions.</p>
<p>-To build confidence in public speaking and presentation.</p>
<p>-To organize inter-departmental debate competitions.</p>
<p>-To invite eminent speakers to share their insights and experiences.</p>
