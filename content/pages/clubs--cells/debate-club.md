## About

## DEBATE CLUB

The debate club provides a dynamic platform for students to develop their public speaking, reasoning, and analytical skills.it encourages participants to engage in thought-provoking discussions and structured debates on contemporary issues, fostering confidence and clarity in expression.

## Objectives

-To enhance communication and persuasive skills.

-To encourage healthy debate and respect for diverse opinions.

-To build confidence in public speaking and presentation.

-To organize inter-departmental debate competitions.

-To invite eminent speakers to share their insights and experiences.
