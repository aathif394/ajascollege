<h2 id="literary-club">Literary Club</h2>
<p>The Literary Club of Al Jamia Arts and Science College is a vibrant platform for students who love to read, write, and express themselves creatively. Our mission is to cultivate a culture of literature within the campus where imagination, intellect, and inspiration come together.</p>
<p>Through our diverse activities, we strive to nurture a generation of readers, thinkers, and writers who can contribute meaningfully to society.</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>– To encourage students to read widely and write creatively.</p>
<p>– To promote literary awareness and appreciation across the campus.</p>
<p>– To build confidence in communication and creative expression.</p>
<p>– To create a community of learners passionate about literature in all its forms.</p>
<h2 id="activities">Activities</h2>
<p>– Literature Talk Series</p>
<p>– Creative Writing Workshops</p>
<p>– Reading Circles</p>
<p>– Literary Competitions</p>
<p>– Book Reviews &amp; Magazine Contributions</p>
