<div class="content-blog-single-wrap migrate-content"><h2 id="about">About</h2>
<h2 id="innovation-and-entrepreneurship-development-centre-iedc">Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centre (IEDC)</h2>
<p>Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centre (IEDC) is a flagship initiative of Kerala<br></br>
Startup Mission to promote innovation and entrepreneurship among the student and<br></br>
academic fraternity in the educational institutions in the State of Kerala</p>
<h2 id="objectives">Objectives</h2>
<p>To promote an innovation driven entrepreneurship culture among the students</p>
<p>To develop and promote commercially viable innovative product and solution from the<br></br>
students.</p>
<p>To promote enterprise among budding technopreneurs and thereby creating more<br></br>
employment opportunities</p>
<p>To bridge the gap between Industry and Academia</p>
<p>To support the budding Entrepreneurs through Grants and to create an<br></br>
entrepreneurship flavour in the academic fraternity.</p>
<img src="/assets/uploads/2024/05/Frame-1.svg" alt="" class="img-fluid">
<h2 id="vision">Vision</h2>
<p>To create an innovation culture among Innovators by introducing them the State-of-the-art<br></br>
technologies and positioning the Institution as a Learning and Innovation Platform by<br></br>
delivering technically competent and skilled Entrepreneurs.</p>
<h2 id="mission">Mission</h2>
<p>To create IEDC as an Innovation Platform and to create future founders by promoting<br></br>
Innovation, Technology and Business Learning among student community</p>
