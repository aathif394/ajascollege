<h2 id="aboutacademic-council">About<br>Academic Council</h2>
<p>The Academic Council is the principal academic body of the Institute and responsible for the maintenance of standards of education, teaching and training, inter departmental co-ordination, research, career guidance, and continuous evaluation etc.</p>
<h2 id="functions-of-the-academic-council">Functions of the Academic Council
</h2>
<p>The Academic Council of the Institute has the following responsibilities :</p>
<ul><li>Maintain academic standards.</li><li>Make arrangements for the conduct of internal examinations.</li><li>Maintain proper standards for the internal examinations.</li><li>Suggest measures for departmental co-ordination.</li><li>Monitor the academic functioning of the Institute, admission, discipline, etc.</li><li>Appoint various committees for the smooth functioning of the institution.</li><li>Consider the recommendations of the committees and to take necessary action.</li></ul>
