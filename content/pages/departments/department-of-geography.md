<h2 id="about">About</h2>
<p>Established in the year 2016 The Department of Geography is committed to provide quality education to aspiring graduates. At present, it has Three Assistant Professors with rich experience in the field of teaching. The department is endowed with a group of well experienced and dedicated faculty members.</p>
<h2 id="vision">Vision</h2>
<p>This programme in particular will impart knowledge in conventional and recent concepts and its applications in various areas of Geography. Further, the programme provides training to students in various practical aspects of Geography. Students will be able to gain unique knowledge and skills necessary to facilitate applications in new emerging professions related to Geography.</p>
<h2 id="mission">Mission</h2>
<p>The mission of a geography department is to advance knowledge and understanding of the Earth’s physical and human environments through research, education, and outreach.</p>

<h2 id="head-of-department">Head Of Department</h2>
<h2 id="mohammed-abdul-ganiyy-a-p">Mohammed Abdul Ganiyy A P</h2>
<h2 id="msc-geography-net">MSc Geography,NET</h2>
<p>Welcome to the Department of Geography at Al Jamia Arts &amp; Science College , Perinthalmanna.</p>
<p>I am pleased to introduce you to our vibrant department, where we explore the dynamic interactions between humans and their environment. The Department of Geography is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and community engagement, preparing students to understand and address critical global challenges.</p>
<p>Our department offers a diverse range of undergraduate  that encompass physical geography, human geography, geospatial technologies, environmental studies, and regional planning. Through interdisciplinary coursework and hands-on fieldwork, students acquire valuable skills in spatial analysis, environmental management, and sustainable development.</p>
<p>Our faculty members are dedicated educators and researchers who are passionate about advancing geographical knowledge and fostering critical thinking among our students. With access to state-of-the-art laboratories, GIS labs, and research facilities, we provide an enriching learning environment that encourages exploration and discovery.</p>
<p>Whether you are interested in studying landscapes, urban environments, climate change, or social and cultural geography, our programs offer a comprehensive foundation for your academic and professional aspirations.</p>
<p>I encourage you to explore our website to learn more about our programs, faculty profiles, research initiatives, and the vibrant community that defines the Department of Geography at Calicut University. Join us as we explore the complexities of our world and contribute to creating a sustainable future.</p>
<p>Warm regards,</p>
<p>Head of Department</p>
