## Career Guidance and Placement Cell

The College always strives its maximum for raising her students to greater heights. In accordance with the vision and mission of the Institution, Career Guidance and Placement Cell has been instrumental in arranging Career counselling sessions and orientation programmes.

Students are guided to participate in various recruitment programmes inside and outside the campus. Students are instructed to register their names in Employment Exchanges and register for one time registration of Kerala Public Service Commission. The opportunities for higher education and job vacancies are displayed on the notice board and announced through public address system.
