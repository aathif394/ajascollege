<div class="col-lg-8"><div class="site-content clearfix"><article class="post post-blog-single"><div class="content-blog-single-wrap migrate-content"><h2>Scholarship</h2>
<p>The following scholarships from different sources are available for deserving students:</p>
<h2>Government of India Scholarships</h2>
<ul><li>National loan scholarships </li><li>National merit scholarships </li><li>Scholarships for physically handicapped students </li><li>Scholarships for SC students. </li><li>Scholarship for Lakshwadeep students </li><li>Central Sector Scholarship </li><li>Inspire scholarship. </li></ul>
<h2>Kerala Government Suvarna Jubilee Scholarships</h2>
<ul><li>State Merit Scholarship </li><li>Fisheries Scholarship </li><li>Scholarships for Muslim and Nadar girl students </li><li>Scholarships for physically handicapped students </li><li>scholarship for proficiency in sports. </li></ul>
<h2>University Scholarship</h2>
<ul><li>University merit scholarship </li><li>University sports incentive scholarship </li><li>Cultural scholarship </li></ul>
<h2>Scholarship Policy</h2>
<p>Provide free education to those students who will miss their higher education due to financial crisis and should be applicable to students of all categories irrespective of religion and community so that GER of this region will be improved.The committee will implement this policy with the view to  provide free education up to at least 20% of the total strength of the institution.This management committee will use an effective system to monitor the genuineness of the policy conduct.This management committee will scrutinise entire applications for the scholarship and will determine the most deserving candidates for the scholarship based on the enquiry conducted by a subcommittee constituted for it.At the end of each semester the heads of every department of this institution will submit the progress report of the free ship holders. The management committee with the presence of educational experts like Mr. Kolathur Mohammed Moulavi,Higher Education, Kerala PSC and the mentor of this committee as well as the first Principal of this institution (1995-2006)Professor K I Mohammed Kutty, could implement the scholarship policy from the year of establishment of this college itself.</p>
<p><strong>Scholarship from other sources</strong></p>
<p>Mammunni Moulavi Foundation Scholarship</p>
<p>This scholarship is distributed to the students of this institution by the college committee on the recommendation of the principal.</p>
