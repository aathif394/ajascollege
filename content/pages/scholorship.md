## Scholarship Policy

Provide free education to those students who will miss their higher education due to financial crisis and should be applicable to students of all categories irrespective of religion and community so that GER of this region will be improved.The committee will implement this policy with the view to  provide free education up to at least 20% of the total strength of the institution.This management committee will use an effective system to monitor the genuineness of the policy conduct.This management committee will scrutinise entire applications for the scholarship and will determine the most deserving candidates for the scholarship based on the enquiry conducted by a subcommittee constituted for it.At the end of each semester the heads of every department of this institution will submit the progress report of the free ship holders. The management committee with the presence of educational experts like Mr. Kolathur Mohammed Moulavi,Higher Education, Kerala PSC and the mentor of this committee as well as the first Principal of this institution (1995-2006)Professor K I Mohammed Kutty, could implement the scholarship policy from the year of establishment of this college itself.
