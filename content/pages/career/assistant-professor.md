<p>As an Assistant Professor, you will be responsible for teaching undergraduate and graduate-level courses, conducting research in your area of expertise, and contributing to the academic community through service and outreach activities.</p>
<p>Key responsibilities include:</p>
<ul><li>Developing and delivering engaging and effective lectures, seminars, and lab sessions</li><li>Designing and updating course curricula to ensure relevance and alignment with program objectives</li><li>Supervising and mentoring students, including thesis and dissertation advising</li><li>Conducting original research, publishing in peer-reviewed journals, and presenting at academic conferences</li><li>Securing external research funding through grants and contracts</li><li>Participating in departmental and university-wide committees and initiatives</li><li>Providing academic advising and career guidance to students</li><li>Engaging in community outreach and service activities</li></ul>
<p>Qualifications:</p>
<ul><li>Earned doctorate degree in the relevant field of study</li><li>Demonstrated excellence in teaching and a commitment to student success</li><li>Strong research record with a growing portfolio of publications</li><li>Ability to work collaboratively with colleagues and contribute to a positive, inclusive academic environment</li><li>Excellent communication and interpersonal skills</li></ul>
<p>If you are a passionate educator and researcher who is eager to make a meaningful impact, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity to join our dynamic faculty.</p>
