Al Jamia Arts and Science College holds the following affiliations, recognitions and accreditations.

## University of Calicut Affiliation

AJAS is affiliated to the University of Calicut for all its undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

## Government of Kerala Recognition

The college is recognised by the Government of Kerala and holds minority institution status.

## NAAC B++ Accreditation

AJAS is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a B++ grade.

## Minority Institution Status

AJAS holds minority institution status, recognised by the Government of Kerala.

## Other Certifications

The college also holds UGC recognition, AICTE approval and ISO certification. See the Mandatory Disclosure menu under About Us for the full set of affiliation and compliance certificates.
