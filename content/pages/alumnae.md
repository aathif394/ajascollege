The AJAS College, Perinthalmanna Alumni Association is a window into the past, a symbol of the present, and a bridge to the future. AJAS College actively engages its alumni in the institution’s growth efforts, creating a strong alumni network and making them a vital stakeholder. The association aids in the institution’s development on the levels of institutional, academic, and infrastructure. Alumni have continually donated to the institute, which has allowed it to carry out programmes that otherwise would not have been viable.
The AJAS College alumni association is one of the largest contributors to the institution’s different developmental initiatives. The Association has branches in the UAE, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and the UK, among other nations. Each year, the chapters identify the deserving and needy students with the institution’s support and offer them the necessary aid. Additionally, the Association hosts a variety of cultural events and contests.
Alumni communicate with students and provide their knowledge and top tips in a certain profession. Additionally, they actively participate in charitable projects like mentoring students in their fields of specialisation. This AJAS Alumni members’ fond reunion provided a platform for developing the cordial ties amongst Alumni throughout the world.

Goals of Alumni:To involve the AJAS community throughout the world in promoting and advancing the college’s greatness.

Mission of Alumni :By establishing ties between former students, current students, and friends of the AJAS college of Education and one another, AJAS Alumni Association promotes a lifetime sense of pride and belonging. The Alumni Association speaks out on behalf of the college and its graduates in a reliable, impartial, and cooperative manner.
