<div class="content-blog-single-wrap migrate-content"><h1>FinNav – Financial Literacy Program</h1>
<img src="/assets/uploads/2026/06/WhatsApp-Image-2026-06-17-at-3.09.59-PM.jpeg" alt="" class="img-fluid">
<h2 id="event-date">Event Date :</h2>
<h2 id="16-06-2026">16/06/2026</h2>
<p>The Department of Commerce &amp; Management Studies, in collaboration with Bharat Skill Development Center and in association with IQAC, successfully organized “FinNav – Financial Literacy Program” on 16 June 2026 at the Seminar Hall of Al Jamia Arts &amp; Science College, Perinthalmanna. The session was led by Mr. Anees K.A., NSE Empanelled Trainer, Financial Advisor &amp; Research Analyst, who delivered an insightful presentation on financial planning, investment opportunities, savings strategies, and wealth creation. The program aimed to enhance the financial awareness of 5th semester students and equip them with practical knowledge for making informed financial decisions. The interactive session witnessed enthusiastic participation from students, who actively engaged in discussions and clarified their doubts regarding personal finance and investment management. The program proved to be highly informative and beneficial, contributing significantly to the financial literacy and future preparedness of the participants.</p></div>
